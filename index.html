About

Born in the early glow of an unheated backyard shed, Boulder-based Jehovah's Fitness Club kindled the warm blend of jazz, Latin, funk, and hip-hop grooves that defines their sound. The seven-piece band brings a fresh, playful take on jazz, pairing danceable rhythms with conversational improvisation and a healthy dose of mirth.

Recent appearances at Larimer Lounge and Lost Lake, alongside a residency at Trident Booksellers & Cafe, have built JFC's reputation for lively, spontaneous performances rooted in musical conversation and feel-good energy.
