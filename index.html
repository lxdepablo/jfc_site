About

Jehovah's Fitness Club is a Denver band that treats every set like leg day — heavy, relentless, and over before you're ready for it to be. Expect a set that moves between crunching riffs and pocket-deep grooves, built to be played loud in small rooms.

Recent reps include a run at Larimer Lounge and a night at Lost Lake, plus an ongoing monthly residency at Trident, where the band has been using the stage as a lab for new material in front of a home crowd.
